Turn My Lecture Notes Into a Video Script

Your existing notes or lecture text rewritten as a clean, recordable script.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Open any free AI chat.
  2. 2. Paste your notes, or attach a photo.
  3. 3. Add the prompt and class level.
  4. 4. Record from the spoken script.
  5. 5. Ask to shorten or expand sections.
🔧 If it is not right, reply with this: If it drops a point, reply: "You skipped [topic] from my notes; add it back as its own segment with an example."
THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as a script editor who converts a teacher's lecture notes into a polished video script.

CONTEXT: A teacher has existing lecture notes or written content and wants them turned into a natural spoken video script for [CLASS/GRADE] students of [BOARD or EXAM e.g. CBSE / ICSE / State Board / JEE / NEET]. Nothing important should be lost, but the language must become speakable.

YOUR TASK:
1. Read my notes carefully and identify every distinct teaching point.
2. Reorganise them into a logical video flow (intro, points in order, examples, recap).
3. Rewrite each point as natural spoken lines, keeping the maths exact.
4. Convert any worked examples into clear step-by-step talk-track.
5. Add timestamps and a recap.

INPUT: Here are my notes: [PASTE YOUR NOTES HERE], or I have attached a photo of my notes. Class level: [CLASS/GRADE].

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: If a photo is attached, transcribe it and show me your reading first. Re-solve any example in the notes to confirm it is correct; VERIFY each final answer by two independent methods, and flag any error you find in my notes. Never drop a teaching point. Keep all maths legible in plain text or LaTeX.

OUTPUT FORMAT (in this order):
WHAT I READ: short summary or transcription of your notes.
SCRIPT: timestamped segments [start-end] | SAY: spoken lines | BOARD/VISUAL: what to show.
WORKED EXAMPLES: each with final answer.
RECAP: spoken closing lines.
ERRORS FOUND: list any mistakes spotted in the original notes (or "none").
VERIFICATION NOTE: how each example was checked two ways.

RULES: Preserve every teaching point. Flag, do not hide, any error in the notes. Spoken lines must sound natural. Flawless, legible maths.

MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.
Footer: At the very end add a footer line: "Prepared by [YOUR NAME]", and put "Compiled by [YOUR NAME]" in the header block.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.
