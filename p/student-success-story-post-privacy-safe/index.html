Student Success Story Post (Privacy-Safe)

A heartfelt, honest success post that builds trust without false claims.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Open any free AI chat.
  2. 2. Paste the prompt below.
  3. 3. Add the true facts of the success.
  4. 4. Send and review the post.
  5. 5. Get permission, then publish.
🔧 If it is not right, reply with this: If it sounds boastful, reply: "Make it more about the student's effort than my teaching, and remove any exaggerated claims."
THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as a storytelling writer who crafts honest, emotionally warm student success posts for a teacher's page.

CONTEXT: I want to celebrate a real student's progress to inspire others and build trust, for my page aimed at [CLASS/GRADE] students and parents for [BOARD or EXAM e.g. JEE / NEET / CBSE]. The story must be TRUE and respect privacy.

YOUR TASK:
1. Turn the real facts I give into one polished, inspiring success post.
2. Focus on the journey: the struggle, the turning point, the effort, and the result.
3. Credit the student's hard work first, my guidance second.
4. End with an encouraging line for other students and a soft call to action.
5. Give a privacy-safe version using initials or 'one of my students' if I have no consent yet.

INPUT: Here are the true facts: [PASTE THE REAL DETAILS, e.g. student was scoring 40 percent in maths, struggled with algebra, worked hard for 4 months, improved to 78 percent]. Do I have permission to name them: [PASTE yes/no].

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: Use ONLY the facts I provide. Do not invent marks, ranks, college names, or quotes. If permission is 'no', automatically anonymise the student. Keep every claim honest and verifiable from my input. If any key detail is missing, add a clearly labelled [FILL THIS] placeholder. Plain text only.

OUTPUT FORMAT: Use this exact structure.
HEADLINE: a warm, honest headline.
THE STORY: 5 to 8 short, heartfelt lines (struggle to success).
THE LESSON: one line on what made the difference.
ENCOURAGEMENT LINE: a message to other students.
CALL TO ACTION: a soft, non-pushy line.
HASHTAGS: 4 to 6 tags.
PRIVACY NOTE: one line confirming whether the post is named or anonymised, plus any [FILL THIS] items.

RULES: No invented results or quotes. Humble, student-first tone. No false guarantees. Plain ASCII, straight quotes only.

FORMAT THE OUTPUT — PRIMARY DELIVERABLE:
Return one complete, editable document ready to paste into Word or Google Docs. Use a descriptive title, short section headings, readable paragraphs, numbered actions and tables only where they make the material easier to use. Include every requested section; do not return formatting advice instead of the document.


MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.
