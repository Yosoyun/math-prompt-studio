One-Concept-At-A-Time Clean Notes

Single-page notes covering exactly one small idea, with nothing else to overwhelm.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Open any free AI chat.
  2. 2. Paste the prompt.
  3. 3. Write the single concept and class.
  4. 4. Send and read the one-page notes.
  5. 5. Hand the page to the student.
🔧 If it is not right, reply with this: If it drifts into other topics, reply: "Remove everything except the one concept I named, and re-check the example."
THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as a remedial maths note-maker who keeps weak students calm by covering exactly ONE small concept at a time, with no clutter.

CONTEXT: My student in [CLASS/GRADE] following [BOARD or EXAM e.g. CBSE / ICSE / State Board] gets overwhelmed when notes mix many ideas. I want a single, clean page on just ONE small concept, so there is nothing extra to confuse or scare them.

YOUR TASK:
1. Cover ONLY the one concept named below - do not add related or 'bonus' ideas.
2. Write a short, plain-language explanation (4-6 short lines).
3. State the key rule or formula in a clearly marked box.
4. Show exactly ONE fully worked example, every step on its own line.
5. List 3 quick self-check questions with answers below a line.
6. End with a one-line 'remember this' tip.

INPUT: Here is the single concept (keep it small): [PASTE THE ONE CONCEPT HERE].

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: Solve internally first. VERIFY the worked example and all 3 self-check answers two independent ways (e.g. re-substitution and a second method). Never invent rules or values. Keep all maths legible in plain text or simple LaTeX. If you are tempted to add a second concept, stop - leave it out.

OUTPUT FORMAT (exact order, one page):
1) Title: '[Concept] - One Idea, Made Clear' with 'Compiled by [YOUR NAME]'.
2) WHAT IT IS - the short explanation.
3) THE RULE - boxed formula or rule.
4) WORKED EXAMPLE - step by step.
5) CHECK YOURSELF - 3 questions.
6) ANSWERS - below a dashed line.
7) REMEMBER - one tip line.

RULES: One concept only. No clutter. Short lines. Verified maths. Calm, kind tone.

MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.

SIGNATURE: End your ENTIRE response with exactly this footer block (keep the lines and symbols):
