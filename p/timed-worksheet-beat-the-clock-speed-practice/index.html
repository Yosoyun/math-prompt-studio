Timed Worksheet (Beat-the-Clock Speed Practice)

A speed worksheet with a target time and per-question time budget, plus a quick answer key.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Name the skill to speed up.
  2. 2. Say the total minutes allowed.
  3. 3. Add class and exam.
  4. 4. Send and check the timing.
  5. 5. Print for timed practice.
🔧 If it is not right, reply with this: If the time feels wrong, reply: "The time budget is unrealistic. Re-estimate each question's seconds for a Class [X] student and adjust the total time."
THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as a mathematics teacher who designs timed speed-practice worksheets to build exam pace.

CONTEXT: I teach [CLASS/GRADE] under [BOARD or EXAM e.g. CBSE / ICSE / JEE / NEET]. Students know the maths but are too slow in exams. I want a timed worksheet on [SKILL/TOPIC] that students must finish within [TOTAL MINUTES] minutes to build speed and accuracy.

YOUR TASK:
1. Create a set of questions on [SKILL/TOPIC] that a prepared student of this level can realistically complete within [TOTAL MINUTES] minutes.
2. Choose the NUMBER of questions to fit the time; for each question give a suggested time budget in seconds.
3. Keep questions short and computational (speed-friendly), increasing slightly in difficulty.
4. Add a clear 'Target: finish in [TOTAL MINUTES] min' banner and a place for the student to record their actual time.
5. Show marks and total.

INPUT: Here is the skill/topic and the total minutes allowed: [PASTE SKILL AND MINUTES HERE].

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: Solve every question internally first and realistically estimate solving time for a student at this level (not for an expert). VERIFY each final answer two independent ways. Make sure the total time budget across questions matches [TOTAL MINUTES]. Never invent formulas or values. Keep all maths fully legible in clean LaTeX or clear plain text.

OUTPUT FORMAT (exact headings, in order):
- TIMER BANNER: 'Target: [TOTAL MINUTES] minutes', Skill, Class, Total Marks, and 'Compiled by [YOUR NAME]'.
- QUESTIONS: numbered, each with its time budget (in seconds) and marks.
- TIME CHECK ROW: 'My actual time: ____ min' line for the student.
- ANSWER KEY: all final answers, clearly separated at the end.

RULES: Time budgets must be realistic for the level and sum to the total. Questions speed-friendly. Single correct answers. Answer key must match. Short wording.

MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.
