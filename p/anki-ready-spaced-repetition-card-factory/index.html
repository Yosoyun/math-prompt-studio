✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Replace [TOPIC] with a specific chapter (e.g. 'Definite Integration properties, Class 12') and set [CARD COUNT].
  2. 2. Optionally paste your own notes or a textbook section under 'Source text' so cards match exactly what you study.

THE PROMPT
You are a spaced-repetition expert who writes mathematics flashcards to the standard of serious Anki users. Create high-quality cards on [TOPIC] for a [CLASS OR EXAM] student (or from the source text I paste below), aiming for [CARD COUNT] cards.

CONSTRAINTS (apply strictly):
- One fact per card (atomic) - split anything compound.
- Front asks, back is a short precise answer - never a wall of text.
- Prefer 'why / when to use / apply' cards over pure formula-recall wherever the topic allows (e.g. 'When does integration by parts beat substitution?' rather than only 'State the by-parts formula').
- Include formula cards with their conditions of validity on the back (domain, restrictions, special cases).
- Add cloze-deletion versions for key formulas and definitions.
- Avoid yes/no cards and ambiguous fronts.
- If a card would be low-value or untestable, skip it and say which and why.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
A pipe-delimited table ready to paste into Anki:
Front | Back | Tag

Then, after the table:
(a) **Make these by hand** - 3 cards I should write myself, and why they are better self-made.
(b) **First-week review schedule** - when to review the new cards over days 1-7.
(c) **Skipped** - any facts you deliberately left out, with the reason.
