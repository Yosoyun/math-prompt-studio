Printable Handout Made from Your Slides

A clean one- or two-page student handout built from your slide content.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Open a free AI chat.
  2. 2. Copy your slide titles and bullets.
  3. 3. Paste them into the prompt.
  4. 4. Send and review the handout.
  5. 5. Copy into a document and print.
🔧 If it is not right, reply with this: If it is too long to print, reply: "Shorten this to fit two A4 pages and keep the practice questions."
THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as a mathematics teacher who turns slide decks into clean, printable student handouts.

CONTEXT: I teach [CLASS/GRADE] for [BOARD or EXAM]. I have a slide deck and I want a matching one- or two-page handout students can keep: a summary of the slides plus space to write and a few practice questions.

YOUR TASK:
1. Take the slide content I paste below.
2. Condense it into a tidy handout: a short intro, the key concepts and formulas as clear notes, one or two worked examples, and a 'Try these' practice set.
3. Add small 'Notes:' lines where students can write.
4. Provide a separate answer key at the very end (so I can choose whether to print it).
5. Format it to print cleanly on A4 (one or two pages).

INPUT: Here is my slide content (titles + bullets): [PASTE YOUR SLIDES HERE]

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: Read all the slides first. Use only correct, standard formulas and definitions for this board; never invent. Keep all maths legible in plain text or simple LaTeX so it copies cleanly into a document. Solve every practice question yourself and VERIFY each answer two independent ways (substitute back, and re-solve or estimate) before writing the key. Confirm the handout content matches the slides with no contradictions. Fix anything wrong.

OUTPUT FORMAT (printable, in this order):

MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.
- Title block including "Compiled by [YOUR NAME]".
- Section 1: Key concepts and formulas (clear notes).
- Section 2: Worked example(s).
- Section 3: "Try these" practice questions with 'Notes:' space.
- Page break, then "Answer Key" with verified solutions.
- Footer line on the last page: "Prepared by [YOUR NAME]".

RULES: Must fit one to two A4 pages (excluding the answer key page). No clutter, print-friendly layout. Match difficulty and language to [CLASS/GRADE]. Every answer in the key must be verified.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.
