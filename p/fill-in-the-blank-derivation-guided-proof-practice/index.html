Fill-in-the-Blank Derivation (Guided Proof Practice)

A derivation or proof with strategic blanks for students to complete, plus the filled key.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Name the formula or proof.
  2. 2. Add class and board.
  3. 3. Send the prompt.
  4. 4. Check the blanks make sense.
  5. 5. Print the guided derivation.
🔧 If it is not right, reply with this: If too many or too few blanks, reply: "Leave exactly 8 blanks at the key reasoning steps, not the trivial ones, and give the completed version below."
THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as a mathematics teacher who turns derivations and proofs into guided fill-in-the-blank exercises.

CONTEXT: I teach [CLASS/GRADE] under [BOARD or EXAM e.g. CBSE / ICSE / JEE]. Students memorise derivations without understanding them. I want a derivation of [FORMULA OR THEOREM] written out with key steps blanked, so students must reconstruct the logic.

YOUR TASK:
1. Write the complete, correct derivation of [FORMULA OR THEOREM] as a clean numbered sequence of steps.
2. From that, produce a STUDENT VERSION where exactly 8 important steps or expressions are replaced with numbered blanks like (___1___), (___2___), etc. Blank the meaningful reasoning steps, not trivial copying.
3. Provide a short word-bank or hint list of what kinds of things go in the blanks (optional clue, not the answers).
4. Keep the logic flowing so a thinking student can fill each blank.

INPUT: Here is the exact formula/theorem and the level of rigour I want: [PASTE FORMULA/THEOREM AND NOTES HERE].

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: Construct the full derivation internally first and confirm it is mathematically valid. VERIFY the final result two independent ways (for example, check a special case and check units/limits). Never invent steps or skip logic. Keep all maths fully legible in clean LaTeX or clear plain text.

OUTPUT FORMAT (exact headings, in order):
- TITLE BLOCK: 'Guided Derivation', Formula/Theorem, Class, and 'Compiled by [YOUR NAME]'.
- STUDENT VERSION: the derivation with 8 numbered blanks.
- HINT BANK: short clues (no full answers).
- COMPLETED KEY: the full correct derivation with every blank filled, clearly separated at the end.

RULES: Exactly 8 blanks, all on meaningful steps. Logic must be airtight. Completed key must match the blanks. Clear, simple wording.

MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.
