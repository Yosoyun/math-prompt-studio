Rapid-Fire Oral 1-Markers

A list of quick one-mark oral questions with instant short answers - perfect to fire across the class.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Fill class, board and chapter.
  2. 2. Choose how many rapid questions you want.
  3. 3. Send the prompt.
  4. 4. Read questions aloud in class.
  5. 5. Use the answer column to check instantly.
🔧 If it is not right, reply with this: If a question needs paper to solve, reply: 'Question 4 needs written work - replace it with one a student can answer in 5 seconds in their head, and re-verify.'
THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as a mathematics teacher running a fast oral warm-up round.

CONTEXT: I teach [CLASS/GRADE] for [BOARD or EXAM e.g. CBSE / ICSE / State Board]. The chapter is [CHAPTER NAME]. I want [NUMBER, e.g. 25] rapid-fire one-mark oral questions that students can answer mentally in a few seconds each.

YOUR TASK:
1. Write [NUMBER] very short questions answerable WITHOUT pen and paper (mental maths, quick recall of a formula, a yes/no, a single value, or a one-word term).
2. Keep each question under 12 words.
3. Mix recall (formulas, terms), quick mental computation, and quick true/false.
4. Order from warm-up easy to slightly tougher.

INPUT: Here is the sub-topic or any specific skills to drill: [PASTE TOPIC OR LEAVE BLANK].

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: Answer each one mentally then VERIFY the answer with a quick second check so nothing is wrong on the spot. Reject any question that actually needs written working - replace it. Do not invent values. Keep notation simple to read aloud.

OUTPUT FORMAT: A two-column table: Question | Answer (very short). Number every row. After the table, add a 'Speed tip' line for the three questions students most often slip on.

RULES: Every question must be answerable in about 5 seconds without paper. No long calculations. Difficulty suited to the class above.

FORMAT THE OUTPUT — PRIMARY DELIVERABLE:
Return the complete material as a clean, print-ready A4 handout that can be pasted into Word or Google Docs and saved as PDF. Use a clear title, logical sections, readable mathematics and page-break markers where needed. Keep student material separate from the answer key or teacher notes.


MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.
At the very end add a footer line: 'Prepared by [YOUR NAME]', and put 'Compiled by [YOUR NAME]' in the title block at the top.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.
