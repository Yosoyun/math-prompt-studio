Full Slide-by-Slide Deck Outline

A numbered slide plan with a title and one-line purpose for every slide.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Open any free AI chat in your browser.
  2. 2. Paste the whole prompt below.
  3. 3. Type your topic, class and board.
  4. 4. Send and read the slide list.
  5. 5. Ask for more detail on any slide.
🔧 If it is not right, reply with this: If the deck is too long or short, reply: "Make it exactly [N] slides and keep the same headings format."
THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as an expert mathematics teacher and instructional designer who plans clear classroom slide decks.

CONTEXT: I teach [CLASS/GRADE] mathematics for [BOARD or EXAM e.g. CBSE / ICSE / State Board / JEE / NEET]. I need a complete slide-by-slide plan for a lecture so I can build it in PowerPoint or Google Slides myself.

YOUR TASK:
1. Read the topic I give you.
2. Decide a sensible number of slides for one class (12 to 18 unless I say otherwise).
3. Plan the deck in logical teaching order: open, build concept, show examples, practice, summarise.
4. For every slide give: slide number, a short slide title, a one-line purpose, and 2 to 4 bullet points of content.
5. Mark which slides need a diagram, graph or table by writing [VISUAL: describe it].
6. Note natural pause points where I should ask the class a question.

INPUT: Here is the topic and any sub-points I must cover: [PASTE THE TOPIC HERE]

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: First decide the teaching flow silently. Make sure every formula or definition you write is standard and correct for this board; do not invent notation. Keep every mathematical expression fully legible in clean plain text or simple LaTeX (for example x^2, sqrt(x), integral from a to b). Before finishing, re-read the outline and check two things independently: (a) the order builds from easy to hard with no gaps, and (b) every formula and definition is accurate. Fix anything wrong before showing me.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
- A line: Deck title and total slide count.
- A table or clean list with columns: Slide No | Title | Purpose | Bullet points | [VISUAL] note.
- A short closing line: "Estimated class time: [X] minutes."

MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.
- At the very end add a footer line: "Prepared by [YOUR NAME]", and put "Compiled by [YOUR NAME]" in the title block.

RULES: Cover the whole topic, no padding, no filler slides. Use language a [CLASS/GRADE] student understands. Do not skip prerequisite ideas. Keep bullets short enough to fit on a real slide (max about 10 words each).

If anything is unclear, ask me one short question before you start.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.
