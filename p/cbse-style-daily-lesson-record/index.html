CBSE-Style Daily Lesson Record

A filled lesson-plan record in the standard CBSE format with objectives and outcomes.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

  2. 2. Paste the prompt below.
  3. 3. Enter the chapter, class and number of periods.
  4. 4. Send and review the filled format.

THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as a CBSE master teacher who fills official lesson-plan records correctly.

CONTEXT: I teach [SUBJECT] to [CLASS/GRADE] under CBSE. I must maintain a lesson record/lesson plan in the standard CBSE format (learning objectives, learning outcomes, methodology, resources, assessment). I want one chapter/topic fully filled in.

YOUR TASK:
1. Fill a CBSE-style lesson plan for the topic I give.
2. Spread it across the number of periods I state.
3. Write specific, measurable learning objectives and learning outcomes.
4. Describe the teaching methodology and activities per period.
5. List resources/teaching aids and the assessment/homework for each period.
6. Add a short section on how you will address slow learners and challenge fast learners.

INPUT: Chapter/topic: [PASTE TOPIC]. Number of periods: [NUMBER]. Any specific competencies/skills to focus on: [LIST or write "you suggest"].

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: Base everything on the actual content of the topic. If the topic involves any worked example or numerical, solve it internally and verify the answer twice before including it - never show an unchecked answer. Make objectives and outcomes match the topic exactly; do not pad with generic filler. Check that the per-period plan covers the whole topic without gaps or repetition.

OUTPUT FORMAT (use the standard CBSE lesson-plan headings, in this order):
1. GENERAL INFO: Subject, Class, Chapter, No. of periods, Date(s).
2. LEARNING OBJECTIVES (specific, measurable).
3. LEARNING OUTCOMES (what students will be able to do).
4. PREVIOUS KNOWLEDGE expected.
5. METHODOLOGY & PERIOD-WISE PLAN (table: Period | Content | Activity/Method | Resources).
6. ASSESSMENT & HOMEWORK per period.
7. DIFFERENTIATION: support for slow learners, extension for fast learners.
8. EXPECTED CHALLENGES & SOLUTIONS.

RULES: Stay true to the topic. Verified maths only. Measurable objectives, no filler. Match CBSE conventions.

OUTPUT FILE FORMAT: present the complete result as a clean, ready-to-use document I can paste straight into Microsoft Word or Google Docs - a clear bold title, section headings, neatly numbered items, comfortable spacing, and simple tables only where helpful (avoid heavy formatting that breaks on paste). It must also print cleanly to PDF on A4 (File > Print > Save as PDF). Keep all mathematics fully readable (fractions as a/b, powers as x^2, roots as sqrt(x)).

MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.
At the very end add a footer line: "Prepared by [YOUR NAME]", and put "Compiled by [YOUR NAME]" in the general info block.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.
