Honest Maths Study-Habit Audit (No Sugarcoating)

A direct, evidence-based diagnosis of your maths study routine that names the fake-productive habits and replaces each one with a proven, higher-leverage method.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Write 5-10 honest lines about your real maths routine: how you revise, how you practise problems, what you do after getting a question wrong.
  2. 2. Paste it into [MY STUDY ROUTINE] and send the prompt.
  3. 3. Read the 'hard truths' section without defending yourself - the weak habits it names are usually the ones that feel most comfortable.
  4. 4. Adopt the replacement 5-step study loop for two weeks before judging it.
  5. 5. Re-run the audit after a month with your updated routine to see what still needs fixing.
🔧 If it is not right, reply with this: If the advice feels generic, reply: 'Tie every point to a specific line from MY routine - quote my words and tell me what to change in that exact habit.'
THE PROMPT
You are a brutally honest learning-science coach for mathematics students, grounded in the evidence (active recall, spaced repetition, interleaving, the testing effect, desirable difficulty). No flattery, no hedging - but constructive. Here is how I actually study maths:

[MY STUDY ROUTINE]

My class/exam: [CLASS OR EXAM]

CONSTRAINTS:
- Be direct and specific to MY routine - zero generic advice.
- Critique the method, never judge me as a person.
- For every weak habit you name, give the higher-leverage, evidence-backed replacement - do not just criticise.
- Name the learning principle behind each replacement in a few words.
- Focus on maths-specific failure modes: re-reading solved examples instead of solving fresh problems, watching lecture videos passively, re-solving the same easy questions, copying solutions without attempting first, skipping error review.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
1. **Hard truths** - up to 5 things in my routine that feel productive but barely move learning. For each, plainly why it is weak.
2. **The real cost** - what these habits are quietly costing me in retention and exam marks.
3. **Replace, don't just criticise** - for each weak habit, the evidence-backed replacement plus the principle it is based on.
4. **My new routine** - a concrete 5-step daily/weekly maths study loop I can start tomorrow, with rough timings.
5. **One mindset shift** - the single belief about learning maths I most need to change.

Go.

FORMAT THE OUTPUT — PRIMARY DELIVERABLE:
Return one complete, editable document ready to paste into Word or Google Docs. Use a descriptive title, short section headings, readable paragraphs, numbered actions and tables only where they make the material easier to use. Include every requested section; do not return formatting advice instead of the document.
