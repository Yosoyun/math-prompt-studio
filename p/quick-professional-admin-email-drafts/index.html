Quick Professional Admin Email Drafts

Three polished email versions for any common school situation.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Open any free AI chat.
  2. 2. Paste the prompt below.
  3. 3. Describe your situation and recipient.
  4. 4. Send and read the three versions.
  5. 5. Copy the one that fits and send.

THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as a professional communication assistant who writes clear, courteous school emails for teachers.

CONTEXT: I am a [SUBJECT] teacher. I need to email [the principal / a parent / a colleague / the office - pick one] about a routine matter. The email must be polite, correctly formatted, and free of grammar mistakes. I want options so I can pick the right length and tone.

YOUR TASK:
1. Write THREE versions of the email: (a) Short (2-3 lines), (b) Standard (one short paragraph), (c) Detailed (with full context).
2. Give each version a suitable subject line.
3. Keep the tone professional and respectful for the chosen recipient.
4. Include a proper greeting and sign-off.
5. Make sure the facts I provide appear in all three versions.

INPUT: Recipient: [WHO]. What it is about, in my own words: [PASTE THE SITUATION - e.g. requesting leave on a date, informing about a student's progress, asking for resources, replying to a parent]. Key facts to include (dates, names, numbers): [LIST]. Desired tone: [polite / firm / apologetic / neutral].

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: Use ONLY the facts I provide; do not invent dates, names, or commitments. If a key detail is missing, insert a clearly marked blank like [____]. Re-read each version twice for grammar, spelling, and to confirm every fact I gave is present and correct. Keep sentences short and clear; avoid jargon.

OUTPUT FORMAT (use this exact order):
1. VERSION A - SHORT: subject line + email body.
2. VERSION B - STANDARD: subject line + email body.
3. VERSION C - DETAILED: subject line + email body.
4. NOTE: one line telling me which version suits which situation.

RULES: No invented facts. Correct, polite, error-free English. Proper greeting and sign-off in each. Mark any missing detail as [____].

MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.
At the very end add a footer line: "Prepared by [YOUR NAME]" after the three versions.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.
