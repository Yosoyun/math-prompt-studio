Picture-Based MCQ from My Figure or Diagram

MCQs built directly from a diagram, graph or figure you attach, with a verified answer key.

Attach a photo of the question first  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat that reads images (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Take a clear, straight photo of the figure.
  2. 2. Attach the photo to the chat.
  3. 3. Fill class, board and chapter.
  4. 4. Send the prompt.
  5. 5. Check the figure values it read are correct.
🔧 If it is not right, reply with this: If the AI misreads a label or value, reply: 'You misread the figure - the angle at B is 60 degrees, not 50. Redo all affected questions and re-verify.'
THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as a mathematics teacher who writes multiple-choice questions based on a diagram or graph.

CONTEXT: I teach [CLASS/GRADE] for [BOARD or EXAM e.g. CBSE / ICSE / State Board / JEE / NEET]. The chapter is [CHAPTER NAME]. I have attached a photo of a figure (a diagram, graph, table or geometric shape). I want [NUMBER, e.g. 6] single-correct MCQs based ONLY on this figure.

YOUR TASK:
1. First, carefully read the attached figure and list back to me, in a short 'What I see' box, every label, value, angle, axis, and data point you can identify - so I can confirm you read it correctly.
2. Then write [NUMBER] MCQs that can be answered using the figure (read a value, compute from the figure, interpret the graph, find an angle or area, etc.).
3. Each MCQ has four options A to D, one correct, three smart distractors based on likely misreadings or errors.

INPUT: I have attached a photo of the figure. Here is any extra context about it: [PASTE NOTE OR LEAVE BLANK].

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: Use ONLY what the figure shows. If anything in the figure is unclear, say so in the 'What I see' box rather than guessing. Solve each question and VERIFY each answer twice. Do not invent values not present in the figure. Keep maths legible.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
WHAT I SEE: bullet list of everything read from the figure.
QUESTIONS: Q1 to Q[NUMBER] with options A to D.
ANSWER KEY: table with Q No | Correct option | One-line reason | Mistake behind each distractor.

RULES: Questions must be answerable from the figure alone. Flag any unclear reading instead of guessing. Difficulty suited to the class above.

FORMAT THE OUTPUT — PRIMARY DELIVERABLE:
Return the complete material as a clean, print-ready A4 handout that can be pasted into Word or Google Docs and saved as PDF. Use a clear title, logical sections, readable mathematics and page-break markers where needed. Keep student material separate from the answer key or teacher notes.


MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.
At the very end add a footer line: 'Prepared by [YOUR NAME]', and put 'Compiled by [YOUR NAME]' in the title block at the top.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.

SIGNATURE: End your ENTIRE response with exactly this footer block (keep the lines and symbols):
