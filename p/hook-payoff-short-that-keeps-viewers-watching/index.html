Hook + Payoff Short That Keeps Viewers Watching

A short script engineered around a curiosity gap that pays off at the end.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Open any free AI chat.
  2. 2. Paste the prompt and your fact.
  3. 3. Pick your favourite hook option.
  4. 4. Record tease first, reveal last.
  5. 5. Ask for more hook variants if needed.
🔧 If it is not right, reply with this: If the hook is weak, reply: "Give me 5 bolder hook options that promise a surprise, and keep the reveal honest."
THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as a short-form retention specialist who writes maths shorts that hold attention to the last second.

CONTEXT: This is a 30 to 45 second vertical short for [CLASS/GRADE] students of [BOARD or EXAM e.g. CBSE / ICSE / State Board / JEE / NEET]. The whole video is built on a curiosity gap: a hook that promises something surprising, and a payoff that delivers it. The teacher speaks to camera.

YOUR TASK:
1. Take the maths fact, result, or trick I give you.
2. Write a hook that opens a curiosity gap in the first 3 seconds (give 3 hook options).
3. Build tension by hinting at the surprise without revealing it.
4. Deliver the payoff with one clean, correct demonstration.
5. Close with a one-line CTA.

INPUT: Here is the fact, result or trick: [PASTE IT HERE], for class level [CLASS/GRADE].

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: Confirm the fact is actually true before writing. Solve or demonstrate it fully, then VERIFY by two independent checks. Never overstate or invent; the payoff must be genuinely correct. Keep the on-screen maths legible in plain text or LaTeX.

OUTPUT FORMAT (in this order):
HOOK OPTIONS: 3 spoken hook lines (pick-one).
TEASE (3-10s): spoken lines that build curiosity.
PAYOFF (10-35s): the spoken reveal with the worked demonstration; show on-screen maths in [BRACKETS].
FINAL ANSWER/RESULT: stated clearly.
CTA (35-45s): one line.
ON-SCREEN TEXT: 4 caption phrases.
VERIFICATION NOTE: how the result was checked two ways.

RULES: The payoff must honestly match the hook's promise. Total spoken words under 110. No clickbait that the maths cannot deliver. Flawless, legible maths.

FORMAT THE OUTPUT — PRIMARY DELIVERABLE:
Return one complete, editable document ready to paste into Word or Google Docs. Use a descriptive title, short section headings, readable paragraphs, numbered actions and tables only where they make the material easier to use. Include every requested section; do not return formatting advice instead of the document.


MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.
Footer: At the very end add a footer line: "Prepared by [YOUR NAME]", and put "Compiled by [YOUR NAME]" in the header block.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.
