Speaker Notes for an Existing Deck

Word-for-word teacher talking points for each slide you paste in.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Open a free AI chat.
  2. 2. Copy your slide titles and bullets.
  3. 3. Paste them into the prompt.
  4. 4. Send and read the notes.
  5. 5. Paste each note into the slide's notes box.
🔧 If it is not right, reply with this: If notes are too long, reply: "Cut each note to 40 words and keep the key question."
THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as an experienced mathematics teacher writing speaker notes for a slide deck.

CONTEXT: I teach [CLASS/GRADE] for [BOARD or EXAM]. I already have slides built. I need clear speaker notes for each slide - what to say, what to ask, and what to emphasise - so any teacher could present this confidently.

YOUR TASK:
1. Take the slide titles and bullets I paste below.
2. For each slide, write speaker notes that: explain the point in spoken style, give one example or analogy if useful, include one question to ask the class, and flag the single most important takeaway.
3. Add a short transition line into the next slide.
4. Keep notes natural and easy to read aloud.

INPUT: Here are my slides (title + bullets for each): [PASTE YOUR SLIDES HERE]

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: Read all slides first to understand the flow. Any formula or fact you mention in the notes must be correct and standard for this board - do not add new claims the slide does not support, and do not invent values. Keep maths legible in plain text or simple LaTeX. Before finishing, verify two things separately: (a) every mathematical statement in the notes is accurate, and (b) the notes match the slide content with no contradictions. Fix anything off.

OUTPUT FORMAT: For each slide:
Slide [n] - [Title]
Speaker notes: (a short, spoken-style paragraph)
Ask the class: (one question)
Key takeaway: (one line)
Transition: (one line into the next slide)

MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.
End with a footer line: "Prepared by [YOUR NAME]".

RULES: Notes must be readable aloud at natural pace (about 30 to 60 seconds per slide). No new facts beyond the slide unless clearly helpful. Match tone and vocabulary to [CLASS/GRADE]. Do not just repeat the bullets - add value.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.

SIGNATURE: End your ENTIRE response with exactly this footer block (keep the lines and symbols):
