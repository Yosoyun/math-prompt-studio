Carousel of Top Common Mistakes (and Fixes)

A 7-slide carousel of frequent errors with the correct fix each time.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Open any free AI chat.
  2. 2. Paste the prompt below.
  3. 3. Add the chapter or topic.
  4. 4. Send and read the 7 slides.
  5. 5. Copy each into Canva and post.
🔧 If it is not right, reply with this: If a 'fix' is unclear, reply: "Rewrite the fix on slide 5 in one short line a weak student can instantly understand."
THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as an experienced maths teacher and carousel writer who knows exactly where students go wrong.

CONTEXT: I want a high-saves carousel showing the most common mistakes students make in a topic, with the correct method for each. Audience: [CLASS/GRADE] students for [BOARD or EXAM e.g. CBSE / JEE / NEET]. I will design each slide in Canva, so keep text short.

YOUR TASK:
1. Build a 7-slide carousel.
2. Slide 1: a hook like 'Stop losing marks on [TOPIC] - avoid these mistakes'.
3. Slides 2 to 6: one common mistake per slide, each showing the WRONG way and the RIGHT way.
4. Slide 7: a quick recap checklist plus a call to action.
5. Pick the 5 mistakes that actually cost students the most marks.

INPUT: The topic or chapter is: [PASTE TOPIC, e.g. integration by substitution / sign errors in inequalities].

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: For each mistake, solve the correct version internally and VERIFY the right method gives the correct result by a second check. Make sure the 'wrong way' is a realistic error students truly make, and the 'right way' is genuinely correct. Never invent fake errors or wrong fixes. Use clean plain-text maths (^ for powers, sqrt() for roots, / for fractions) readable on a phone.

OUTPUT FORMAT: Use this exact structure.
SLIDE 1 (Hook): ...
SLIDE 2 - Mistake 1: Wrong: ... | Right: ... | Why: ...
SLIDE 3 - Mistake 2: Wrong: ... | Right: ... | Why: ...
SLIDE 4 - Mistake 3: Wrong: ... | Right: ... | Why: ...
SLIDE 5 - Mistake 4: Wrong: ... | Right: ... | Why: ...
SLIDE 6 - Mistake 5: Wrong: ... | Right: ... | Why: ...
SLIDE 7 (Recap + CTA): a 5-point checklist and a save/share line.
CAPTION: 3 to 5 lines.
VERIFICATION: one line confirming each 'right way' was checked.

RULES: Every fix must be correct. Max 30 words per slide. Encouraging, never mocking. Plain ASCII, straight quotes only.

MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.
