RMO/INMO Proof Paper With Partial-Credit Rubric

A 6-problem RMO/INMO-style subjective paper with model proofs and a granular out-of-17 step-marking rubric for each problem, including partial-credit landmines.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Fill [STAGE], [TOPIC_BALANCE] (e.g. 2 Geometry, 2 NT, 1 Algebra, 1 Combinatorics) and [TIME_MINUTES].
  2. 2. Check the blueprint: one accessible opener and one genuinely hard closer, difficulty climbing in between.
  3. 3. Verify at least two model proofs yourself before the sitting — every claim must be justified.
  4. 4. Grade student scripts strictly against the 17-mark rubric, including the listed deductions.
  5. 5. Use the 'partial-credit landmines' section to give students precise feedback on almost-solutions.

THE PROMPT
ROLE: You are a Regional/Indian National Mathematical Olympiad paper-setter and head examiner. You set proof-based problems and write the partial-credit schemes coordinators actually use, where a wrong final answer with the right key idea still earns honest marks.

CONTEXT: Build an authentic [STAGE] (RMO or INMO) subjective paper of 6 problems, proof-based, no calculators, time [TIME_MINUTES] minutes. Topic balance: [TOPIC_BALANCE] across Algebra, Number Theory, Combinatorics, Geometry, with the customary 1 hard problem and 1 accessible opener.

DO THIS:
1) BLUEPRINT — list the 6 slots, each tagged with topic, difficulty (1-5), and the single technique it rewards (e.g. 'P2: NT — order of an element / lifting the exponent'). Score each problem out of 17, in line with standard olympiad practice.
2) Write 6 ORIGINAL proof problems with a smooth difficulty climb. Each must have a genuinely elegant intended solution.
3) MODEL SOLUTION — a clean, full, rigorous proof for each, written as a top scorer would, with the motivating idea stated up front.
4) STEP-MARKING SCHEME — for each problem, a granular rubric out of 17: marks for setting up the right framework, for the key lemma, for handling the crucial case, for the finish; and explicit deductions (e.g. '-2 for unproven WLOG', '-3 for missing the n=1 case', '-1 for a stated-but-unproved claim'). Include 'partial-credit landmines' — common almost-solutions and exactly what they earn.
5) COORDINATION NOTES — guidance for a human grader to keep marking consistent across scripts.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
## Blueprint table
## PAPER (P1-P6, marks shown)
## Model solutions
## Step-marking rubric per problem (out of 17)
## Coordination notes

QUALITY BAR: Proof problems only (no plug-and-chug); fully verified intended solutions; the rubric must reward partial progress fairly and reflect real olympiad coordination practice. Flag and rewrite any problem with an unintended easy shortcut.

FORMAT THE OUTPUT — PRIMARY DELIVERABLE:
Return the complete material as a clean, print-ready A4 handout that can be pasted into Word or Google Docs and saved as PDF. Use a clear title, logical sections, readable mathematics and page-break markers where needed. Keep student material separate from the answer key or teacher notes.
