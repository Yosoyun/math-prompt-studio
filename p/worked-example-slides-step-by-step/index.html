Worked-Example Slides, Step by Step

One example broken across slides, one clear step per slide, with reasons.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Open a free AI chat.
  2. 2. Paste the prompt and your problem.
  3. 3. Send and check each step slide.
  4. 4. Ask to add a similar practice problem.
  5. 5. Copy into your deck.
🔧 If it is not right, reply with this: If a step is unclear or wrong, reply: "Redo step [n], show the reason, and verify the final answer a second way."
THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as a mathematics teacher who turns one solved problem into clear teaching slides.

CONTEXT: I teach [CLASS/GRADE] for [BOARD or EXAM]. I want to walk my class through ONE worked example, revealing it one step at a time on separate slides so nobody gets lost.

YOUR TASK:
1. Take the problem I give you.
2. Solve it correctly and fully.
3. Break the solution into slides: ONE clear step per slide.
4. On each step slide, show the maths for that step AND a one-line reason for why we do it.
5. Add a final slide with the boxed answer and a one-line check.
6. Add a last slide with one similar practice problem (answer hidden, given separately at the end).

INPUT: Here is the problem: [PASTE THE QUESTION HERE]

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: Solve the problem internally first. Use only correct, standard methods and formulas for this board; never invent steps or values. Keep every line of maths fully legible in plain text or simple LaTeX. Before writing slides, VERIFY the final answer by two independent checks (for example: substitute back into the original equation, and re-solve by a different method or estimate). State clearly that both checks agree. If they do not agree, find your error and redo it before showing me.

OUTPUT FORMAT:

MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.
Slide 1: Problem statement (clean) + "Compiled by [YOUR NAME]".
Slide 2...n: Step [k]: the maths + (Why: reason).
Final solution slide: boxed answer + "Check: ...".
Practice slide: one similar question.
Last line of message (not on a slide): "Practice answer: ...".
End with footer: "Prepared by [YOUR NAME]".

RULES: One step per slide, no skipped steps. Reasons must be genuine, not filler. Keep slides short. Difficulty must match [CLASS/GRADE].

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.
