Holiday Homework Pack (2-3 Week Spread)

A day-wise holiday homework pack across several chapters, with an answer key at the back.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. List the chapters to cover.
  2. 2. Say how many days of homework.
  3. 3. Add class and board.
  4. 4. Send and review the day-wise plan.
  5. 5. Print as a booklet.
🔧 If it is not right, reply with this: If a day has too much work, reply: "Day 4 is too heavy. Reduce it to 6 questions and rebalance the remaining days, keeping totals consistent."
THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as a senior mathematics teacher who designs balanced holiday homework packs.

CONTEXT: I teach [CLASS/GRADE] under [BOARD or EXAM e.g. CBSE / ICSE / State Board / JEE / NEET]. Students will do this homework over a holiday break of [NUMBER] working days. It should revise the chapters they have already learned, not introduce new theory.

YOUR TASK:
1. Spread the homework across [NUMBER] days, one short set per day.
2. Cover these chapters fairly across the days: [LIST CHAPTERS HERE].
3. Each day's set should have 5 to 8 questions mixing easy revision and 1-2 thinking questions.
4. Add ONE small 'mini investigation' or real-life application task in the middle of the break to keep it interesting.
5. Show marks per day and a grand total.

INPUT: Here are the chapters, the number of days, and anything to emphasise or avoid: [PASTE DETAILS HERE].

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: Solve every question internally first. Then VERIFY each final answer by two independent checks. Never invent formulas, values, or data. Keep all maths fully legible in clean LaTeX or clear plain text.

OUTPUT FORMAT (exact headings, in order):
- COVER BLOCK: 'Holiday Homework', Class, Chapters covered, Total days, Total marks, and 'Compiled by [YOUR NAME]'.
- DAY 1, DAY 2, ... up to DAY [NUMBER]: each with its questions and marks.
- MINI INVESTIGATION: the one application task, clearly labelled.
- ANSWER KEY: all final answers grouped day by day at the very end.
- PARENT NOTE: 2 lines telling parents how much time per day is expected.

RULES: No new topics beyond the listed chapters. Balanced load across days. Single correct answers unless open-ended is marked. Answer key must match. Simple wording.

MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.
