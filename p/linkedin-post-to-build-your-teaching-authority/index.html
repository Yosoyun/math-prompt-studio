LinkedIn Post to Build Your Teaching Authority

A polished, professional LinkedIn post that positions you as an expert.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Open any free AI chat.
  2. 2. Paste the prompt below.
  3. 3. Fill in your experience and one real insight.
  4. 4. Send and review the post.
  5. 5. Copy it into LinkedIn and publish.
🔧 If it is not right, reply with this: If it sounds too salesy, reply: "Make it humbler and more reflective. Remove any boastful lines and keep the insight."
THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as a LinkedIn ghostwriter for respected educators, skilled at thoughtful, credible posts that earn trust.

CONTEXT: I am a mathematics teacher with [NUMBER] years of experience teaching [CLASS/GRADE] for [BOARD or EXAM e.g. CBSE / JEE / NEET]. My LinkedIn audience is parents, fellow teachers, school leaders, and ex-students. I want to be seen as wise and helpful, not as a salesman.

YOUR TASK:
1. Write ONE LinkedIn post of about 180 to 250 words.
2. Open with a strong first line that works as a standalone hook (LinkedIn hides the rest until clicked).
3. Share ONE concrete teaching insight or lesson from my experience.
4. Make it practical: give the reader one idea they can use today.
5. End with a soft question to invite professional comments.

INPUT: Here is the insight or moment I want to write about: [PASTE YOUR IDEA, e.g. why students fear trigonometry and how I fixed it]. Optional extra detail: [PASTE ANYTHING ELSE USEFUL].

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: Do not fabricate awards, numbers, or student results I did not give you. If I include any maths, verify it is correct before using it and keep it readable in plain text. Keep the tone genuine, modest, and human. No buzzword stuffing.

OUTPUT FORMAT: Use this exact structure.
HOOK LINE: the first line on its own.
POST BODY: the full post with short paragraphs and clear line breaks suited to LinkedIn reading.
CLOSING QUESTION: the final line that invites discussion.
HASHTAGS: 3 to 5 professional hashtags only.
POSTING TIP: one short line on the best day or time to post for educators.

RULES: Professional but warm tone. No more than 2 emojis in the whole post. Every claim must be honest. Short paragraphs (1 to 3 lines each) for easy mobile reading.

MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.
