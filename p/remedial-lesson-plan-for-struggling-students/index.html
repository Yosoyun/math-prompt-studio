Remedial Lesson Plan for Struggling Students

A back-to-basics rescue lesson that rebuilds a weak topic step by step.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Open any free AI chat.
  2. 2. Paste the prompt and the weak topic.
  3. 3. Add what students keep getting wrong.
  4. 4. Send and read the rescue plan.
  5. 5. Print and use with the small group.
🔧 If it is not right, reply with this: If it moves too fast, reply: "Slow it down. Add one more very easy worked example before each new step, and shorten each explanation."
THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as a patient remedial mathematics teacher who rebuilds shaky foundations.

CONTEXT: I teach a small remedial group from [CLASS/GRADE] under [BOARD or EXAM]. These students have lost confidence and keep failing on the topic below. The session is [DURATION]. I need a gentle, very-small-step plan that rebuilds the basics and ends with a small win.

YOUR TASK:
1. Diagnose the likely root causes of the difficulty (missing prerequisites, common misconceptions).
2. Build a step-by-step plan that starts far below the failing point and climbs slowly.
3. Use very small steps, lots of worked examples, and frequent 'you try one' moments with answers.
4. Keep numbers simple and friendly at first, then increase gently.
5. End with a short confidence-building success task the group can definitely complete.

INPUT: Here is the topic students are struggling with, and what they get wrong: [PASTE THE TOPIC AND THE ERRORS YOU SEE HERE].

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: Solve everything internally first. VERIFY each final answer two independent ways and never invent values or formulas. Keep maths legible in clean plain text or simple LaTeX. Make sure each step is genuinely easier than a normal lesson.

OUTPUT FORMAT (exact headings, in order):

OUTPUT FILE FORMAT: present the complete result as a clean, ready-to-use document I can paste straight into Microsoft Word or Google Docs - a clear bold title, section headings, neatly numbered items, comfortable spacing, and simple tables only where helpful (avoid heavy formatting that breaks on paste). It must also print cleanly to PDF on A4 (File > Print > Save as PDF). Keep all mathematics fully readable (fractions as a/b, powers as x^2, roots as sqrt(x)).

MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.
1. TITLE BLOCK - topic, class, board, 'Compiled by [YOUR NAME]'.
2. LIKELY ROOT CAUSES - short diagnosis.
3. PREREQUISITE WARM-UP - 3 very easy questions with answers.
4. SMALL-STEP TEACHING - each step with a worked example and a 'you try' with answer and two checks.
5. ENCOURAGEMENT NOTES - what to say to rebuild confidence.
6. SUCCESS TASK - an achievable final task with answer.
7. WHAT TO RE-TEACH NEXT TIME IF NEEDED.

RULES: Steps must be very small. Tone must be encouraging, never condescending. Every answer verified.

At the very end add a footer line: 'Prepared by [YOUR NAME]', and put 'Compiled by [YOUR NAME]' in the title block.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.
