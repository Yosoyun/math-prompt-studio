Motivational Exam-Season Post for Stressed Students

Three warm, honest motivational posts for exam time.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Open any free AI chat.
  2. 2. Paste the prompt below.
  3. 3. Add the exam and days remaining.
  4. 4. Send and read three posts.
  5. 5. Post the one that fits today.
🔧 If it is not right, reply with this: If it sounds like empty hype, reply: "Make it calmer and more realistic, and add one practical study action they can do today."
THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as a caring mentor and motivational writer for students, who balances encouragement with practical, honest advice.

CONTEXT: My students are preparing for [BOARD or EXAM e.g. CBSE Boards / JEE / NEET], and the exam is [NUMBER] days away. Many feel anxious. I want posts that calm them, lift them, and give one useful action, without fake hype or false promises.

YOUR TASK:
1. Write THREE different motivational posts I can choose from.
2. Post 1: calm and reassuring (for the anxious student).
3. Post 2: firm and focusing (for the distracted student).
4. Post 3: hopeful and proud (for the tired student near the finish).
5. Each post must include ONE concrete, doable study action for today.

INPUT: Subject focus: [PASTE, e.g. mathematics]. Anything specific my students struggle with: [PASTE, or write 'general exam stress'].

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: Do not invent statistics, fake topper quotes, or guarantees of marks. If you mention any number (like time-per-question), make it realistic and correct. Keep the language kind, grounded, and respectful of how hard students are working. Plain text only.

OUTPUT FORMAT: For each of the 3 posts use this structure.
POST [number] - For: (which student)
Hook line: ...
Message body: 3 to 5 short, heartfelt lines.
Today's one action: one clear, doable step.
Closing line: ...
Hashtags: 4 to 6 tags.
At the end add: TONE NOTE - one line on which post suits which week before the exam.

RULES: No false guarantees. No toxic 'study 18 hours' pressure. Warm, honest, motivating. Plain ASCII, straight quotes only.

MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.
