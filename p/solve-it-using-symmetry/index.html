YOUR TASK:
1. Find the correct final answer to the question below.
2. STANDARD METHOD: solve straightforwardly, numbered steps.
3. SYMMETRY METHOD: identify the symmetry (swapping variables, mirror points, pairing terms, symmetric functions, etc.), state it clearly, and use it to reach the answer with much less work.
4. Explain the one cue that revealed the symmetry.
