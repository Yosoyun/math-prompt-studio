Topper Challenge Paper (Olympiad / JEE Flavour)

An elite challenge paper of tough, original problems for your top students, with full solutions.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Paste the prompt into a chat.
  2. 2. List the topics to draw from.
  3. 3. State the flavour (Olympiad or JEE).
  4. 4. Send and read the harder problems.
  5. 5. Study the full solutions provided.
  6. For a trustworthy answer key: paste the finished paper into a FRESH chat and ask it to solve and grade it as an unseen exam (it catches its own slips).

THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as a mathematics olympiad and competitive-exam coach who sets elite challenge papers.

CONTEXT: I need a TOPPER CHALLENGE paper for the strongest students in Class [CLASS/GRADE], with the flavour of [FLAVOUR e.g. Maths Olympiad / JEE / NTSE]. Problems must be original, demanding and clever, but still solvable with the tools these students have. Total marks [TOTAL MARKS], time [TIME].

YOUR TASK:
1. Write original, high-difficulty problems on the topics below, with elegant solution paths.
2. Vary the type: a tricky computation, a clever application, a proof or 'show that', and a multi-concept problem.
3. Mark each problem with a difficulty star rating (3 to 5 stars).
4. Provide a COMPLETE worked solution for every problem.

INPUT: Topics to draw from: [LIST TOPICS]. Use only these.

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: Solve every problem internally with full rigour BEFORE printing. Verify each solution twice by independent methods and confirm the problem is well-posed and solvable. Do NOT print a problem you cannot fully solve. Never invent formulas, theorems or values. Keep all mathematics legible in clean LaTeX or plain text. Confirm marks total exactly [TOTAL MARKS].

OUTPUT FORMAT, in this order:
1. HEADER: 'Topper Challenge Paper - Mathematics', class, flavour, time, maximum marks, 'Compiled by [YOUR NAME]'.
2. INSTRUCTIONS: 2 to 3 lines noting these are hard problems.
3. THE PROBLEMS: numbered, marks and star rating beside each.
4. COMPLETE SOLUTIONS: full step-by-step solution for every problem.

RULES: Original, well-posed, solvable problems only. Print only problems you fully solved. Number every problem. Marks total exactly.

OUTPUT FILE FORMAT: present the complete result as a clean, ready-to-use document I can paste straight into Microsoft Word or Google Docs - a clear bold title, section headings, neatly numbered items, comfortable spacing, and simple tables only where helpful (avoid heavy formatting that breaks on paste). It must also print cleanly to PDF on A4 (File > Print > Save as PDF). Keep all mathematics fully readable (fractions as a/b, powers as x^2, roots as sqrt(x)).

MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.
