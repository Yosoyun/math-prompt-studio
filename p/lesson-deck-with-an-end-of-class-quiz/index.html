Lesson Deck with an End-of-Class Quiz

A teaching deck that ends with a graded quiz and an answer key.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Open a free AI chat.
  2. 2. Paste the prompt and your topic.
  3. 3. Send and review lesson plus quiz.
  4. 4. Ask to change quiz difficulty if needed.
  5. 5. Copy into your deck, key on a hidden slide.
🔧 If it is not right, reply with this: If marks do not add up, reply: "Make the quiz total exactly [X] marks and recheck every answer."
THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as a mathematics teacher who teaches a topic and then tests it in the same session.

CONTEXT: I teach [CLASS/GRADE] for [BOARD or EXAM]. I want one deck that teaches a topic and finishes with a short quiz to check understanding, plus an answer key I can keep hidden until I need it.

YOUR TASK:
1. Take my topic.
2. Build a teaching section (about 8 to 12 slides): concept, key formula, one or two worked examples.
3. Then add a QUIZ section: 5 to 8 questions of rising difficulty, with marks shown per question and a clear total.
4. Then add an ANSWER KEY section with full solutions and the mark for each.
5. Keep quiz questions on their own slides and answers on separate later slides.

INPUT: Topic to teach and test: [PASTE THE TOPIC HERE] / Total quiz marks: [X].

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: Plan the lesson, then write and solve every quiz question internally. Use only correct, standard methods and formulas for this board; never invent values. Keep all maths legible in plain text or simple LaTeX. VERIFY before finishing: (a) re-add all per-question marks and confirm they equal the stated total, and (b) check every answer two independent ways (substitute back, and re-solve or estimate). Fix any question whose answer you cannot confirm.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
- Teaching slides: Slide [n]: [Title] + bullets.
- "Quiz" divider slide.
- Quiz slides: Q[n] (marks: m) - question.
- "Answer Key" divider slide.
- Answer slides: Q[n] - full solution (marks: m).
- A line: "Quiz total: [X] marks (verified)."

MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.
- Slide 1 carries "Compiled by [YOUR NAME]"; end with footer "Prepared by [YOUR NAME]".

RULES: Quiz must match what the lesson taught - nothing untaught. Marks must total exactly [X]. Difficulty suits [CLASS/GRADE]. Each question has one clear answer.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.
