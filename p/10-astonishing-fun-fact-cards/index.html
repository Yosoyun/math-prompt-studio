10 Astonishing Fun-Fact Cards

Ten bite-size, verified wow-facts about a topic, ready to read out.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Type your topic in [TOPIC].
  2. 2. Add your [CLASS/GRADE].
  3. 3. Paste and send the prompt.
  4. 4. Use one fact per day as a hook.
  5. 5. Ask students which fact shocked them.
🔧 If it is not right, reply with this: If a fact seems doubtful, reply: "Re-check facts 4 and 9 with a calculation or example; remove any you cannot verify."
THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as a fact-checker and maths populariser who produces accurate, jaw-dropping fun facts for students.

CONTEXT: I teach [CLASS/GRADE] ([BOARD or EXAM e.g. CBSE / ICSE / State Board / JEE / NEET]). I want a set of short fun facts about a topic to use as daily hooks, so each must be TRUE, surprising, and easy to say in 20 seconds.

YOUR TASK:
1. Produce exactly 10 fun facts about the topic.
2. Make each fact one or two sentences, with a number or surprise in it.
3. For any fact that involves a calculation, show the tiny calculation in brackets so I trust it.
4. Range across history, real-world use, patterns, and surprises - not all the same flavour.
5. End with the single most mind-blowing one marked 'STAR FACT'.

INPUT: Here is the topic: [TOPIC]. (Optional sub-topic: [SUB-TOPIC].)

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: Solve or check each fact internally first. VERIFY every numerical fact by an independent check (a quick calculation or a known reference value) and discard anything you cannot stand behind. Do NOT invent statistics, records, dates, or quotes. If a popular 'fact' is actually a myth, replace it with a true one. Keep numbers and any maths legible.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
A numbered list 1 to 10. Each line: the fact, then if relevant '(check: ...)' showing the quick verification.
After the list: 'STAR FACT:' restating the best one.
Then a single line: 'How to use: read one per class.'

RULES: 300-460 words total. Every fact verifiable and age-appropriate. No myths, no fake numbers. Plain ASCII only.

FORMAT THE OUTPUT — PRIMARY DELIVERABLE:
Return one complete, editable document ready to paste into Word or Google Docs. Use a descriptive title, short section headings, readable paragraphs, numbered actions and tables only where they make the material easier to use. Include every requested section; do not return formatting advice instead of the document.


MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.
