Paper Plus a Separate Clean Answer-Key Document

Two clearly separated documents: a clean student paper first, then a standalone answer key.

Works on any free AI  ·  Best tool:  Any AI chat (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Copilot)

✅ How to use this effectively

  1. 1. Paste the prompt into a chat.
  2. 2. Fill in class, marks and chapters.
  3. 3. Send and find the clear divider.
  4. 4. Copy the paper part for students.
  5. 5. Keep the key part for yourself.
  6. For a trustworthy answer key: paste the finished paper into a FRESH chat and ask it to solve and grade it as an unseen exam (it catches its own slips).
🔧 If it is not right, reply with this: If the answers leak into the student paper, reply: 'Remove all answers from the student paper section. Keep the paper totally answer-free, and put every answer only in the separate key.'
THE PROMPT
ROLE: Act as a mathematics paper-setter who delivers a clean student paper and a separate answer key.

CONTEXT: I need TWO clearly separated parts for Class [CLASS/GRADE], [BOARD or EXAM e.g. CBSE / ICSE / State Board]: PART 1 is a clean student question paper with NO answers anywhere, and PART 2 is a standalone ANSWER-KEY document I can print separately. Total marks [TOTAL MARKS], time [TIME].

YOUR TASK:
1. Write a complete, sectioned student paper covering the chapters below, with marks beside every question and absolutely no answers or hints.
2. Insert a clear divider line that reads '===== END OF QUESTION PAPER - ANSWER KEY BELOW (TEACHER COPY) ====='.
3. After the divider, write a standalone answer key that repeats each question number and gives the final answer plus a short working outline for long questions.
4. Make the two parts copy-paste separable.

INPUT: Chapters or topics to cover: [LIST CHAPTERS or TOPICS]. Use only these.

HOW TO WORK IT OUT: Solve every question internally first, then verify each final answer twice by independent methods. Check that the student-paper part contains zero answers or hints. Never invent formulas or values. Keep mathematics legible in clean LaTeX or plain text. Confirm marks total exactly [TOTAL MARKS].

OUTPUT FORMAT, in this order:
1. PART 1 - STUDENT PAPER: header block (paper title, class, board, time, maximum marks, 'Compiled by [YOUR NAME]'), instructions, then the answer-free sectioned paper.
2. THE DIVIDER LINE exactly as written above.
3. PART 2 - ANSWER KEY (TEACHER COPY): question number, final answer, short outline for long ones.

RULES: The student paper must be fully answer-free. The key must cover every question. Stay inside the listed syllabus. Number every question. Marks total exactly.

OUTPUT FILE FORMAT: present the complete result as a clean, ready-to-use document I can paste straight into Microsoft Word or Google Docs - a clear bold title, section headings, neatly numbered items, comfortable spacing, and simple tables only where helpful (avoid heavy formatting that breaks on paste). It must also print cleanly to PDF on A4 (File > Print > Save as PDF). Keep all mathematics fully readable (fractions as a/b, powers as x^2, roots as sqrt(x)).

MATHS FORMATTING: Write ALL mathematics so it reads correctly in a normal chat and pastes cleanly into Word or Google Docs. Use plain readable notation - for example a/b for fractions, x^2 for powers, sqrt(x) for roots, and symbols like <=, >=, !=, +-, pi, theta, integral and sum. Do NOT output raw LaTeX or code such as \frac{}{}, \sqrt{}, x^{2}, $...$ or \( ... \) - these appear as broken symbols to a teacher in a free chat.

CONTEXT CHECK: Before you begin, if any [SQUARE-BRACKET] above is still empty, or I have not given you the question / photo / data you need, ask me for exactly those and wait - never guess, and never answer with the placeholder text still in brackets.

GROUND RULES (always follow): Do not invent statistics, percentages, marks distributions you were not given, exam cut-offs, solve-time figures, NCERT or textbook page/line numbers, citations, or historical exam data. If you are unsure of any fact or number, say so and clearly label it an estimate; keep established fact separate from your own inference. Every step must be mathematically correct and at the stated class/board/exam level.

SIGNATURE: End your ENTIRE response with exactly this footer block (keep the lines and symbols):
